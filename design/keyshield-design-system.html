03 — Components

Components

Interactive specimens. Type in the field to drive the live strength meter — analysis runs entirely in your browser, mirroring the production zxcvbn-ts path. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Password field · strength meter · live
Weak ~0 bits · 0 chars

Requirements checklist · NIST 800-63B
  • – At least 15 characters (8 with MFA)
  • – Reads like a passphrase, not a word
  • – Not a common or breached password
  • – No keyboard walks or sequences
  • – No long repeated runs

Composition rules (upper/lower/symbol) are intentionally not required — length and uniqueness win.
